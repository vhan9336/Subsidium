The five replays

Keys 1 to 5 run a landing through both brains.

1 Nominal baseline
All three witnesses agree. The hazard map picks a landing site away from the boulders.
2 HAKUTO-R M1 ispace, April 2023
Passing a crater rim, the measured altitude rose sharply. The software filtered out the correct reading and the lander fell from about 5 km.
3 Resilience ispace, June 2025
The laser rangefinder degraded and valid readings came too late for the lander to slow down.
4 Odysseus Intuitive Machines, Feb 2024
The laser rangefinders never fired. The lander touched down with sideways speed and tipped over.
5 Athena Intuitive Machines, Mar 2025
Altimeter interference and long shadows near the south pole. Athena landed on its side in a crater.
