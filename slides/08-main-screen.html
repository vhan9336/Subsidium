Reading the main screen

One picture on the left. One sentence and three numbers on the right.

  • The sentence at the top right says what is happening now. Its bar is green when things are fine, amber for a warning and red for a problem.
  • The three numbers are the true altitude, what the legacy gate believes, and what Subsidium believes. The small figure beside each is its error.
  • On the altitude tape over the picture, the white line is the true altitude, red is the legacy gate, green is Subsidium, and the dots are the three witnesses.
  • A dot that goes grey has been outvoted.
