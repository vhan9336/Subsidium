The camera witness

Altitude = time to contact × speed.

  • Time to contact (τ) is how long until the lander reaches the ground at its current speed.
  • The camera finds it from how fast the image grows between two frames: τ = Δt ÷ (scale − 1).
  • Multiplying by the speed from inertial gives an altitude.
  • If the image has too little contrast, the camera reports a low lock quality and abstains rather than guess.
