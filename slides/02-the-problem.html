The problem

With two sensors, a disagreement has no answer. With three, it does.

  • Two sensors that disagree leave you guessing which one to believe.
  • HAKUTO-R M1 flew past a crater rim and the true altitude jumped. The software called the correct reading abnormal and filtered it out.
  • The lander believed it was on the surface while about 5 km up, ran out of propellant and fell.
