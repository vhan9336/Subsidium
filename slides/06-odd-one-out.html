Finding the odd one out

The sensor that appears in both failing pairs is the one that is wrong.

All three pairs agreeFuse all three readings.
One sensor fails with both others, and the other two agreeExclude that sensor.
Inertial fails, but rangefinder and camera agreeThe ground really changed. Re-anchor inertial to the new altitude.
All three pairs failNobody can be blamed. Assume the closest ground.
The rangefinder gives no echoCheck inertial against the camera.
