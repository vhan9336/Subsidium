What is real and what is not

The footage is real. The sensor readings are only as real as the mission published them.

  • Each mission replay descends over NASA's orbiter image of where that lander came down.
  • Blue Ghost footage carries NASA's on-screen altitude labels, so all three witnesses run on real Moon footage.
  • Perseverance and DART have no usable altitude record, so they show camera tracking only.
  • For the crash missions, sensor readings are a scaled reconstruction of the published failure, not mission telemetry.
