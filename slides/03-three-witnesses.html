The three witnesses

Each witness measures altitude in a different way, so they rarely fail in the same way.

Inertial
Adds up acceleration since the last known position. Smooth, but it drifts the longer it goes uncorrected.
Rangefinder
A laser pointed at the ground. Accurate, but it can lag, freeze or go silent.
Camera
Watches the ground grow in the image and works out altitude from that. No map or laser needed, but it needs a good view.
