The vote

Every pair of sensors gets a test: are these two close enough to be measuring the same thing?

  • For each pair, r² = (difference between the two altitudes)² ÷ (their combined uncertainty).
  • The result is averaged over half a second. If it stays under the limit of 9 (three sigma), the pair agrees.
  • There are three pairs: inertial and rangefinder, inertial and camera, rangefinder and camera.
  • A verdict has to hold for 0.6 s before the software acts on it, so one noisy frame cannot flip it.
